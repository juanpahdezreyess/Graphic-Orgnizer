🔑 9 Essential Strategies

Eight keys from the manual + one additional recommendation.

1
📚

Increase Your General Knowledge of English

Read, listen, and speak English every day to build vocabulary, grammar, and comprehension skills.

2
🎯

Know Your Strengths and Weaknesses

Identify the areas that need improvement and focus your study time strategically.

3
⏰

Make the Most of Your Preparation Time

Create a study schedule and use short, focused sessions without distractions.

4
📝

Be Familiar with the TOEIC Format

Learn the structure and directions of each section to avoid surprises on test day.

5
✏️

Know How to Mark Your Answer Sheet

Fill circles completely and carefully to prevent alignment mistakes.

6
🎲

Always Guess

There is no penalty for incorrect answers, so never leave a question blank.

7
🧠

Use the Process of Elimination

Remove unlikely options to improve your chances of selecting the correct answer.

8
😌

Control Test Anxiety

Arrive early, breathe deeply, and trust your preparation to stay calm and focused.

9
🏆

Take Practice Tests Regularly

Simulate real exam conditions to improve timing, confidence, and overall performance.

TOEIC® Success Formula:
Practice consistently, manage your time wisely, stay calm, and answer every question.
