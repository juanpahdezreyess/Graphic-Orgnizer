1

Increase Your General Knowledge of English

Read, listen, and practice English daily to improve vocabulary, grammar, and comprehension.

2

Know Your Strengths and Weaknesses

Identify the areas where you need improvement and focus your study time on them.

3

Make the Most of Your Preparation Time

Create a study schedule, use focused sessions, and review consistently with minimal distractions.

4

Be Familiar with the TOEIC Format

Learn the structure, directions, and sections of the test so there are no surprises.

5

Know How to Mark Your Answer Sheet

Fill circles completely and carefully to avoid alignment mistakes during the test.

6

Always Guess

There is no penalty for wrong answers, so never leave any question unanswered.

7

Use the Process of Elimination

Discard clearly incorrect options to increase your probability of choosing the right answer.

8

Control Test Anxiety

Arrive early, stay calm, breathe deeply, and trust your preparation.

9

Take Practice Tests Regularly

Simulating real exam conditions helps build confidence, improve timing, and identify mistakes.
